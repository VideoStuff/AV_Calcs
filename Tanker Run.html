🛢️ TANKER RUN

STRAIT OF HORMUZ

You're a big, slow oil tanker trying to sail through the Strait of Hormuz — and Iranian and US ships and planes are firing missiles and bullets at you from every side!
Your point-defense guns are weak, but they can shoot incoming missiles and bullets out of the air. Scoop up Barrels of crude from the wreckage. When you're sunk, spend them at the shipyard on a tougher tanker, then sail again to get further. Each leg adds new dangers: rammer boats, bombers, shield ships, EMP jammers, submarines and landing ships.
Beware the A-10 Warthogs: their cannon rounds can't be shot down — dodge the red line or hide behind an island!

Steer: drag with finger/mouse (1:1) or WASD / arrows — islands and oil rigs are solid, ramming one wrecks your hull and you must back off   Guns: auto-aim at the nearest missile, bullet or ship inside your dotted range ring (short range!) — tap / click / Space repeatedly to shoot faster   Turbo: TURBO button / Shift   Perks: pick 1 of 3 after every leg   Checkpoints: every 15 legs you reach a new stretch of sea — enemies get 2× tougher, and you can restart from there after you sink   Abilities: unlock Smoke / Salvo / Repair at the shipyard or as perks, then use the round buttons (or keys 1-3)   Combos: chain kills for bonus barrels   Power-ups: grab floating orbs (S/P/R/L) for timed ammo upgrades
