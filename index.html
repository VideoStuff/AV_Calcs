Lenses & shift

LensThrow ratioH shiftV shiftH shift (ft)V shift (ft)

Highlighted lenses cover your throw ratio. "Near wide end" or "Near tele end" means you're close to that lens's limit: within 0.15 throw ratio for the ET-D75LE20 and longer lenses, or the outer 10% of the range for shorter zooms. A lens where you sit closer to the middle leaves room to adjust on site. Throw ratios are WUXGA/4K figures from Panasonic. Shift is how far the image centre can move from the lens centre, as a percentage of image width (H) or height (V); the feet columns apply that to your image size. ET-D75LE50 and ET-D3LEW50 have no lens shift.
