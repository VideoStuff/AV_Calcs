Typing a model from the device list fills in its wattage. Power factor of 1 is the default. Device wattages are maximum ratings.

Leg loads assume each item is plugged in to keep the legs as even as possible: 120V items go on one leg (A, B or C), 208V items across a pair (A–B, B–C or C–A). A few large items can't be split evenly, so the busiest leg can be well above the balanced figure. Spread gear the same way on site, or the busiest leg will be higher still.

Highlighted lenses cover your throw ratio. "Near wide end" or "Near tele end" means you're close to that lens's limit: within 0.15 throw ratio for the ET-D75LE20 and longer lenses, or the outer 10% of the range for shorter zooms. A lens where you sit closer to the middle leaves room to adjust on site. Throw ratios are WUXGA/4K figures from Panasonic. Shift is how far the image centre can move from the lens centre, as a percentage of image width (H) or height (V); the feet columns apply that to your image size.
